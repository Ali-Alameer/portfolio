## Citation figures

Whenever you update the Google Scholar citation figures (`scholarStats` and per-paper
`citations` in `src/data/profile.ts`), also set `scholarStats.lastUpdated` to the date of
the update, written like "9 October 2026". It shows as "Last updated: …" under the
citation chart. Use the live figures from the Scholar profile (`profile.links.scholar`);
if they can't be read reliably, ask the owner for them instead of guessing.
